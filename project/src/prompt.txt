Do not make a broader legal statement than the source supports.

- conditions;
- exceptions;
- limitations;
- jurisdictional qualifications;
- definitions;
- temporal qualifications.
For example, if the source states that a requirement applies "in certain circumstances", do not conclude that it applies in all circumstances.
